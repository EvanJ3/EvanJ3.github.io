Evan Jones

Senior Data Scientist  ·  Machine Learning Engineer

Most people call me EJ. I'm a Senior Data Scientist at FanDuel, where I build the foundational personalization and recommendation models behind the Sportsbook, Picks, and Predicts apps.

I came to machine learning the long way around through finance. A BBA in Finance from the University of Georgia, then an MS in Computational Data Analytics at Georgia Tech. Before FanDuel I spent two and a half years as a Principal ML Engineer at Mercedes-Benz, architecting production systems like Hyperlocal and the patent-pending ECE.
