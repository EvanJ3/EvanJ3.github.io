So this isn't a list of trophies. It's a working library the fiction that shaped how I think, the non-fiction I keep returning to, and the textbooks I actually reach for. A few favorites first, then the whole shelf.

The Shortlist

Favorites

Fiction

Six I'd press on anyone

A Gentleman in Moscow

Amor Towles

My favorite novel a semi-exiled aristocrat lives out the rise of the Soviet Union without ever leaving a single hotel.

The Last Wish

Andrzej Sapkowski

The book that pulled me into the Witcher the source material outdoes both the games and the show.

Anna Karenina

Leo Tolstoy

Even with the ending spoiled, Tolstoy's characters carry every page. No one captures the human condition better.

Animal Farm

George Orwell

The true essence of Orwell a whole critique of Stalinism folded into a simple story of farm animals.

War and Peace

Leo Tolstoy

Knocked off my bucket list early in the pandemic; it sparked a lasting love of Russian literature.

Fathers and Sons

Ivan Turgenev

A timeless portrait of the generational divide that still reads like it was written today.

Non-Fiction

The ones I reread

The Incerto Collection

Nassim Taleb

Five books of skepticism informed by probability funny, rigorous, and quietly ruthless with false expertise.

Permanent Record

Edward Snowden

A candid account that makes the integrity and courage whistleblowing demands unmistakably clear.

Mathematics and its History

John Stillwell

Math as a centuries-long continuum of discovery the historical context cemented my intuition.

A Brief History of Time

Stephen Hawking

I assumed it was overhyped. It isn't space-time and black holes made genuinely intuitive.

Upheaval

Jared Diamond

A framework for how nations rebuild after crisis, told through Diamond's gift for narrative history.

War on Peace

Ronan Farrow

The case for diplomacy its history, its people, and its uncertain future.

Textbooks

The desk reference

AI: A Modern Approach

Russell & Norvig

The bible of AI dense, but it reads surprisingly easily and traces the whole arc of the field.

Deep Reinforcement Learning

Miguel Morales

Got me through GT's RL course the author, our lead TA, explained it better than the lectures did.

Convex Optimization

Boyd & Vandenberghe

Sacred text for anyone in optimization rigorous, thorough, and endlessly referenced.

Introduction to Algorithms

Cormen et al.

My go-to reference for algorithms and data structures, built around real intuition.

High Performance Python

Gorelick & Ozsvald

Speed as a process, not an afterthought how to get the most out of Python.

Linear Algebra Done Right

Sheldon Axler

Finally, eigenvalues without determinants the book that gave me intuition for linear algebra.
